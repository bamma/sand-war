SAND WAR

Bury your rivals. Don't get buried.

Bots:
A / D move   W / Space jump
Mouse aim   Left click fire (hold for water)
1 2 3, Q / E or wheel switch element
Sand clumps bury and stun on a direct hit. Water turns sand to sticky mud. Wind blasts explode dry dunes (not mud).
If your head stays under sand you run out of air. Last one standing wins.
R restart   P pause   Esc menu
